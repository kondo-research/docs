# Research

Kondo Research Institute works along three lines, with Existence Theory at the centre.

## Existence Theory

Formalizing what it means "to exist" uniquely yields a single equation, *V = −H*. It contains no free parameters. Even so, the 26 physical constants of the Standard Model are reproduced from it with no adjustable parameters. The same equation also gives structure: exactly three fermion generations, three spatial dimensions, and the gauge group SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1).

- Full text of the paper: [Physics from Existence I](../paper-1/README.md)
- Overview on the website: [Existence Theory](https://www.kondo-research.org/en/research/et/physics)

## Quantum computing

Quantum cryptanalysis and post-quantum cryptography, including Shor's algorithm applied to the elliptic-curve discrete logarithm problem, and quantum error correction.

- Overview on the website: [Quantum computing](https://www.kondo-research.org/en/research/it/quantum-computing)

## Distributed computing

Research on distributed information access and distributed computing built on the web and the internet, including browsers and blockchain.

- Overview on the website: [Distributed computing](https://www.kondo-research.org/en/research/it/internet)
