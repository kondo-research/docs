## What it establishes

Three axioms about existence — that it is bivalent, that it is uncertain, and that non-existence is forbidden — uniquely determine a single equation, *V = −H*, as an algebraic identity. The paper shows that the mathematical structure obtained by expanding that equation corresponds to the structures of the Standard Model, and that the 26 physical constants of the Standard Model are reproduced from it with no adjustable parameters. The nineteen derived in this paper agree with the established measured values to 0.0006%–2.6%.

Alongside the constants come structural results: exactly three bound states, identified with the three fermion generations; three spatial dimensions; and the gauge group SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1) from the finite geometry built on that number. The paper also gives falsifiable predictions, including the absolute neutrino masses with no oscillation input and strictly zero neutrinoless double-beta decay.

Every numerical result is reproducible with the verification code published alongside the paper. No experimental value enters any derivation; measured values appear only in the comparison columns.
